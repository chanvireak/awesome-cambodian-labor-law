# 13. Overseas Employment & Recruitment Agencies

**Classification rule.** Add new regulations here if they concern Cambodian workers going abroad to work, or the agencies that place them: private recruitment agencies, overseas deployment procedures and travel documents, and skills/language testing for work in another country. (Inbound foreign labour belongs in category 7.)

Part of the [Awesome Cambodian Labour Law](../README.md) index.
