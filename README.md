# Awesome Lists: Cambodian Labor Law & Regulations

A curated index of Cambodian labour-law instruments. Entries are kept in sync with the [Paperless-NGX](http://10.10.10.113:8000) labour-law library (MLVT and NSSF corpora).

**Columns:** each row gives the *Date*, *Issue No.*, *Short Title*, a one-line **Summary** to help you judge at a glance whether the instrument is the one you need, and its *Status*.

**Source:** summaries are derived from the regulation's own text in Paperless. Rows marked `— (not in Paperless)` are older instruments that are not (yet) held in the Paperless library.

**Status legend:** *Active* = in force · *Aborted/Abrogated* = repealed · *Superseded* = replaced by a later instrument · *Expired* = period-based (e.g. an annual wage or holiday calendar) · *Pending* = not yet adopted.
